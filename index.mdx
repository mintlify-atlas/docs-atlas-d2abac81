Bitcoin Wallet is an open-source Android app that lets you send and receive Bitcoin directly on the Bitcoin peer-to-peer network. There is no account to create, no server to trust, and no company holding your funds. Your private keys are stored only on your device.

## Get started in minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Download Bitcoin Wallet from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.schildbach.wallet) or [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.schildbach.wallet_test/). See [Installation](/installation) for all options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your wallet">
    Open the app. A new HD wallet is automatically created on first launch — no sign-up required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back up your wallet">
    Go to **Options → Safety → Back up wallet** and save an encrypted backup to Google Drive or another storage provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or receive bitcoin">
    Tap **Request** to share your address or QR code, or tap **Send** to pay any Bitcoin address or URI.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Bitcoin Wallet uses Testnet by default in development builds. Production builds downloaded from Google Play connect to Bitcoin Mainnet.
</Note>
